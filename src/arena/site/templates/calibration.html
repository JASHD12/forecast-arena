How to read a chart

Each dot groups forecasts with similar probabilities (x) and shows how often those questions actually resolved YES (y). Dots below the diagonal mean over-confident about YES; above it, under-confident.

The numbers

ECE (expected calibration error) is the average gap to the diagonal. The Brier score splits into reliability (calibration error, lower is better), resolution (how well forecasts separate YES from NO, higher is better) and uncertainty (how hard the questions were).

Scope: {{ scope }}. Bins with fewer than 10 forecasts are merged with a neighbour.
