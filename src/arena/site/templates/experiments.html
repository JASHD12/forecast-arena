Probabilistic accuracy of the electricity model ({{ r.day }})

The model predicts a full distribution (13 quantiles) for each daily price statistic. Pinball loss scores that distribution (EUR/MWh, lower is better; about half the CRPS). Coverage shows how often the real value fell inside the central 80% and 90% bands: a well-calibrated model is close to 0.80 and 0.90.

Conformal prediction intervals ({{ r.day }})

Conformalized quantile regression: the margin that makes the 80% and 90% bands hold their coverage on the previous 28 days is added to both ends, then coverage is measured on the evaluation window. Target: 0.80 and 0.90. The promoted model has been refitted on recent days, so coverage on this window can look better than on truly unseen days.
