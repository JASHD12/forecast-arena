Why you can trust the numbers

  • {{ icon("lock") }}

    Locked before the facts

    Every forecast is committed to an append-only git ledger and sealed with a SHA-256 hash chain before the 09:30 UTC lock. Nothing can be edited later.

  • {{ icon("target") }}

    Scored honestly

    Proper scoring rules, calibration checks and day-block bootstrap intervals. Below 30 days, everything says "too early to tell".

  • {{ icon("open") }}

    Fully open

    Code, data, prompts and model versions are public. Anyone can re-verify the whole record with one command.
