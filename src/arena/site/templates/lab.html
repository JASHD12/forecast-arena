How the lab works

  • Pre-registered. The hypothesis, the data windows, the metric and the decision rule are committed before the run.
  • No leakage. Training days come before validation days, which come before the frozen test days.
  • Same yardstick. Brier score on identical questions with a day-block interval, next to the base rate and the gradient-boosted model.
  • Capped cost. All paid calls of the lab together stay under US$2.00 ({{ "%.2f"|format(spent) }} spent so far).
