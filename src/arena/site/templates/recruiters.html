What it is

Every morning the system creates yes/no questions about tomorrow from public data (electricity prices first). Baselines, a gradient-boosting model, LLM forecasters and two LLM agents each publish a probability before the answer exists, into an append-only, hash-chained public record. The next day the answers arrive and every forecast is scored with proper scoring rules. It runs entirely on GitHub Actions and Pages for about one US dollar a month of AI usage.

Measured so far: see the backtests (a year of replayed past days, clearly labelled as not live), the LLM evaluation (it found and fixed a real bug) and the live leaderboard, which says "too early to tell" until the statistics can decide.
